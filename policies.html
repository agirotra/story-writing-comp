Terms & Conditions

These terms govern entry to the Bukmuk Short Story Writing Competition 2026 ("the Competition"), organised jointly by Bukmuk Library and Bukmuk Publishing, brands operated by iKitaab Web, a partnership firm registered in India at BW31A, Shalimar Bagh, Delhi ("Bukmuk", "we", "us"). By paying the entry fee and submitting a story you accept these terms.

Eligibility

  • Open to children aged 6 to 17 years as on 31st August 2026.
  • Entrants must submit in the category matching their age: Whispers of the Wild (6–8), Twisted Fairytales (9–11), Dystopia Hopepunk (12–17).
  • Entries must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian on the child's behalf.
  • There is no restriction on nationality or place of residence. Physical prizes and gifts are shipped within India only — see Shipping & Delivery.

Entry requirements

  • Entry fee is ₹490 per story. A child may submit more than one story; each requires a separate registration and a separate fee.
  • Stories must be original work written entirely by the entrant.
  • Use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or similar) to write or edit the story is not permitted. We may ask for drafts or a short verbal discussion of the story if authorship is in doubt.
  • Plagiarised entries, entries outside the stated word count, and entries in the wrong age category may be disqualified without refund.
  • Stories must be submitted as a single .docx or PDF file, maximum 10MB.
  • Entries must be received by 11:59 pm IST on 31st August 2026.

Judging and results

  • Entries are assessed by the Bukmuk Publishing editorial team and invited jury members on originality, storytelling, use of the theme and age-appropriate craft.
  • The jury's decision is final and binding. We do not enter into correspondence about individual scores or rankings.
  • The top 10 stories in each of the three categories (30 stories in total) will be selected for publication in the Bukmuk Anthology.
  • Results are announced on @bukmuklibrary and by email to the address given at registration.
  • We reserve the right to select fewer than 10 stories in a category if the standard of entries does not warrant it.

Publication rights

  • The author keeps copyright in their story.
  • By entering, the parent or guardian grants Bukmuk Publishing a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to publish the story in the Bukmuk Anthology in print and digital editions, and to use extracts and the child's first name and age in promotional material relating to the Competition and the anthology.
  • Authors of selected stories receive 2 complimentary copies of the anthology. No royalty or fee is payable to entrants.
  • We may lightly copy-edit selected stories for spelling, grammar and house style. We will not change the substance of a story without asking.
  • Full names, school names, photographs and contact details are never published without separate written permission.

General

  • Participation does not guarantee selection for the anthology. Certificates and literary gifts are assured for all valid paid entries.
  • We may amend the deadline or these terms where necessary. Material changes will be published on this page and emailed to registered participants.
  • We may cancel or suspend the Competition in circumstances beyond our reasonable control. If we do, paid entry fees will be refunded in full.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Delhi.

Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum information needed to run the Competition, judge entries, deliver certificates and gifts, and publish the anthology. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • About the child: name, age, story category, story title and the story file itself.
  • About the parent or guardian: name, email address, mobile number, postal address, and Instagram handle if you choose to give it.
  • Payment: a Razorpay payment reference and the amount. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details — payment is handled entirely by Razorpay.
  • Website usage: anonymous, aggregated visit statistics via Google Analytics, used only to understand how many people reach and complete the form.

How we use it

  • To register and judge the entry, and to contact you about it.
  • To deliver certificates, literary gifts and author copies to your postal address.
  • To email you about this Competition, its results and the anthology launch.
  • To send a reminder if you start a registration but do not complete payment. You can opt out by replying to that email.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Registration details are held in a private Google Sheet, and story files in a private Google Drive folder, both restricted to the Bukmuk organising team.
  • Story files are not publicly accessible. They are shared with jury members individually for the purpose of judging.
  • Payment records are held by Razorpay under their own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Registration and contact details are retained for 24 months after the Competition closes, for results, delivery and anthology administration. Story files of selected entries are retained as part of the publishing record. Unselected story files are deleted 24 months after the Competition closes. You may ask us to delete your data sooner.

Children's data

Entries are made by a parent or guardian, who provides consent on the child's behalf at the point of registration. We collect no more information about a child than the name, age and story needed to run the Competition, and we never publish a child's full name, school, photograph or contact details without separate written permission.

Your rights

Email helpdesk@bukmuk.com to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw publishing consent, or withdraw an entry. We respond within 30 days. Withdrawing an entry after judging has begun does not entitle you to a refund — see Refund & Cancellation.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

In short: the ₹490 entry fee is non-refundable once your story has been submitted, because judging and gift fulfilment begin immediately. Duplicate and failed payments are always refunded in full.

When we refund in full

  • Duplicate payment — you were charged twice for the same entry.
  • Failed or incomplete registration — money was debited but no entry was recorded and we cannot reconstruct it.
  • Cancellation before submission — you paid but ask to withdraw before your story file has reached us.
  • We cancel the Competition for any reason.

When we do not refund

  • After your story has been submitted and your entry recorded — judging effort and gift fulfilment begin at that point.
  • If an entry is disqualified for plagiarism, AI-generated content, wrong age category or breaching the word count.
  • If a story is not selected for the anthology. Selection is never guaranteed; certificates and literary gifts are assured for every valid paid entry.
  • If you give an incorrect or incomplete delivery address and the gift cannot be delivered.

How to request a refund

Email helpdesk@bukmuk.com with your Razorpay Payment ID and the child's name, or WhatsApp Shefali on +91 81302 86286. We acknowledge within 2 working days. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days, and may take a further few days to appear depending on your bank.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

What is delivered

  • Every valid paid entry: a certificate of participation and assured literary gifts.
  • Selected authors: 2 complimentary copies of the Bukmuk Anthology.

When

  • Certificates and literary gifts are dispatched after judging concludes, within 30 to 45 days of the results announcement.
  • Anthology author copies are dispatched after the book is printed, following the launch. We will email selected authors with the expected timeline.
  • Delivery within India typically takes 5 to 10 working days after dispatch.

Where

Physical items are shipped to the postal address you give at registration, within India only. Entrants outside India are welcome to compete and will receive their certificate digitally by email; please contact us to arrange anthology copies.

Charges and address accuracy

There is no separate shipping charge — it is included in the ₹490 entry fee. Please check your address carefully at registration. If a parcel is returned because the address was wrong or incomplete, we will contact you, and re-dispatch may be chargeable. To correct an address, email helpdesk@bukmuk.com as early as possible.

Lost or damaged parcels

Tell us within 15 days of the expected delivery date and we will replace the item at no cost.
